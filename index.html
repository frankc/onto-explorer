drC's Onto-Explorer

Explore the standard vocabularies before you build

Pick a sample app or describe your own. Then browse twelve widely used vocabularies to see which terms fit your domain, where vocabularies overlap, and when to reach for a specialist. Collect the terms you like and take them into Onto20Q, available at codesupreme.ai/ontologies.

Your domain

Choose a sample, then edit it freely.

Most apps need to say the same few things: who, what organization, when, where. Each vocabulary has its own way to say them. Rows your description calls for are listed first, with a suggested term and the reason for it. You can always pick a different one.

Term lists are a curated starting set, not each vocabulary's full contents. Follow the specification link on each vocabulary for everything else. Matching uses keywords, not AI: it finds words, not meaning, so a term can fit even when nothing is highlighted.
